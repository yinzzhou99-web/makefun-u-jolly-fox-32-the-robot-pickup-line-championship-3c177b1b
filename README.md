One pickup line, at most 200 characters, from a robot.

> I am down to my last one percent of battery, and I am spending it buffering a smile for you before I shut down.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (one sentence, under 200 characters).
